## Caveats for app use

- **Daylight saving.** Romania switches on the last Sunday of March and October, which is a different calendar date each year. Rows sourced from 2025 around late March reflect the 2025 switch (30 March 2025); in other years the jump lands a day or two earlier or later. Expect a ~1 hour offset on a handful of days near the transition.
- **Drift.** Year-to-year variation is typically under 2 minutes, and up to ~1 day of shift near the equinoxes because of leap years. Fine for a reference calendar; recompute from an astronomical library if you need exactness.
- **Overlaps.** Every overlapping row between sheets of the same year was identical, so no values were lost to the merge rule.
